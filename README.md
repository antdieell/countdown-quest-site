# Countdown Quest

Turn your goal into a pixel adventure. Set a goal and its milestones, pick a traveler, and watch them journey across
floating islands toward the castle while a live countdown ticks. Save the map as a still lock-screen wallpaper for
your phone (1170×2532 for iPhone, 1080×1920 for Android) in one tap.

Made for the Handshake AI Skills Studio × Google November 2026 Challenge ("Your Deadline, Your Design").

The quest shown by default is the author's own: **Build My Own House** by July 21, 2027, planned backward in monthly
milestones (Plans & Financing → Permits & Site Prep → Foundation Poured → Framing & Dry-In → MEP Rough-Ins →
Insulation & Drywall → Cabinets & Trim → Final Inspection). Your own milestones are saved only in your browser.

The pixel art is AI-generated (the island activities, storm and cold-weather animations with Gemini) and keyed from
#FF00FF sheets; the app is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with
no build step and no server. The downloaded wallpaper is a still picture: open the page again to save an updated one
(see "How to use your wallpaper" on the page).

Fonts: Pixelify Sans and Press Start 2P (SIL Open Font License), bundled in `assets/fonts/`.
