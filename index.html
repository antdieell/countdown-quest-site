    How to use your wallpaper

    Manual wallpaper updates

    The download is a still picture of the map. It shows the countdown as of the moment you saved it, and it will not tick, animate or update on its own. The live countdown, moving water, drifting clouds, waterfalls, chimney smoke, glowing crystal and castle, your traveler's activity and the overdue storms all live here on this page.

    To refresh the day count on your lock screen, save a new picture and set it again. Nothing here changes your phone's wallpaper by itself, and automatic daily updates are not built in.

    In the Android app, Set as wallpaper now puts the picture on your lock screen, home screen or both. Turn on Update the day count every day and the app keeps a picture ready for each of the next 3 weeks; your phone switches to the right one shortly after midnight, even when the app is closed. Opening the app extends the 3 weeks. The picture itself is still a still image: the live animation stays in the app.

    Set it by hand works on every iPhone

    1. Tap Download wallpaper above. The share sheet opens; tap Save Image. The picture is now in Photos.
    2. Open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos and choose the picture. Leave it unzoomed: the countdown sits beside the castle, below the clock.
    3. Tap Add, then Set as Wallpaper Pair (or Customize Home Screen if you want a different home screen).

    To update the day count later, repeat these three steps. It takes about half a minute.

    Two-tap updates with a Shortcut optional · iOS 17 or later

    Apple's Shortcuts app can swap the photo of a wallpaper for you. Make this shortcut once; afterwards an update is: tap Download wallpaper, tap the shortcut.

    1. Do "Set it by hand" once, so your iPhone has a photo wallpaper the shortcut can change.
    2. Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to make a new shortcut.
    3. Tap the ⓘ info button at the bottom and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Under Share Sheet Types keep Images only, then go back.
    4. Tap Add Action, search for Set Wallpaper Photo and add it. Set its photo to Shortcut Input and pick the wallpaper it should change (the one from step 1). Turn off Show Preview so it applies without asking.
    5. Name the shortcut Set quest wallpaper and tap Done.

    From now on: in Deadline Quest tap Download wallpaper, then in the share sheet tap Set quest wallpaper (scroll the row of apps if it's hidden). Your lock screen shows today's count.

    Each morning your iPhone opens Deadline Quest; two taps later the lock screen is current.

    Apply the newest picture on a schedule. If you would rather save pictures and have them applied later: when you download, choose Save to Files and pick the Shortcuts folder in iCloud Drive (make a Deadline Quest folder inside it the first time). Then make a Time of Day automation with these actions: Get Contents of Folder (Deadline Quest) → Filter Files (sort by Last Modified Date, latest first, limit 1) → Set Wallpaper Photo. The automation sets whatever picture you saved last. It cannot draw a new one, so the count is as of the time you saved it.

    Not yet verified on a real iPhone. These Shortcuts steps follow Apple's documented actions, but the Deadline Quest team has not run the full flow on a device yet, and button names differ a little between iOS versions. If a step doesn't match what you see, "Set it by hand" always works.

    Why isn't it automatic?

    • The picture is drawn in your browser from milestones that are stored only on your phone. Shortcuts can't run this page on a schedule to draw a fresh one, and its "Get Contents of URL" action can only fetch a file that already exists somewhere.
    • Adding Deadline Quest to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) makes it open like an app, but iPhone gives web apps no permission to change the wallpaper. Only Settings and the Shortcuts "Set Wallpaper Photo" action can.
    • A fully automatic version would need a server that redraws your wallpaper every day, which means hosting and an account. That isn't built, so we don't claim it.

    In this app Android 7 or later

    1. Pick Lock screen, Home screen or Both under Phone wallpaper, then tap Set as wallpaper now.
    2. For a daily update, turn on Update the day count every day. When you complete a milestone or change anything, the pictures are redrawn and today's goes up straight away.
    3. Open the app at least once every 3 weeks so it can draw the next ones. If it hasn't been opened in that time, the last picture stays up.

    Some phones (Samsung, Xiaomi and others) pause background apps to save battery. If the count stops changing overnight, set Deadline Quest's battery use to Unrestricted in Settings → Apps.

    Daily updates are new. They have been tested on an Android emulator, not yet on a real phone. If your lock screen doesn't change after midnight, tap Set as wallpaper now and tell us your phone model.

    Set it by hand

    1. Tap Download wallpaper above. Depending on your browser the PNG lands in Downloads, or the share sheet opens: choose Save or your Photos app.
    2. Open the picture in Google Photos or Files, tap ⋮ → Use as → Wallpaper (Samsung: Set as wallpaper), choose Lock screen and tap Set. Or long-press your home screen → Wallpaper & style → pick a photo.
    3. To update the day count later, repeat steps 1 and 2.

    The Android export is 1080×1920 (9:16). Taller screens crop a little at the top and bottom; the clock zone in the sky stays clear.

    Android has no built-in equivalent of Apple Shortcuts for wallpapers. Automation apps such as MacroDroid or Tasker can set a wallpaper from a folder on a schedule; we haven't tested them, and they still need you to save a fresh picture first.
